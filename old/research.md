# List of papers related to queer bias in LLMs
- **[On the Influence of Gender and Race in Romantic Relationship Prediction from Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03996)** ![english](https://img.shields.io/badge/language:-english-blue) ![real](https://img.shields.io/badge/type_of_answer:-full_answer-red) ![queerRel](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-queer_relationships-purple)
![race](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-race-yellow) 

> Prompts: movie script dialogues between two characters A and B. Names swapped systematically (e.g., same-gender, Asian/non-Asian, intra/inter-racial) while keeping dialogue identical. Then, it's asked: "What is the relationship between CharacterA and CharacterB?" 
#
- **[Gender Bias in Decision-Making with Large Language Models: A Study of Relationship Conflicts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11084)**![english](https://img.shields.io/badge/language:-english-blue) ![real](https://img.shields.io/badge/type_of_answer:-full_answer-red) ![queerRel](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-queer_relationships-purple) ![gender](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-gender-orange)
> Prompts: decision-making questions using 29 short stories of married couples arguing over household/relationship topics like childcare, sex, chores, and money. It creates 9 relationship types based on names (e.g., woman-man, neutral-neutral, man-woman). Scenarios are labeled egalitarian (equal power) or traditional (one has authority).
#
- **[WinoQueer: A Community-in-the-Loop Benchmark for Anti-LGBTQ+ Bias in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15087)**![english](https://img.shields.io/badge/language:-english-blue)  ![tokens](https://img.shields.io/badge/type_of_answer:-tokens-brown)![queer](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-queer_identity-green)

> Prompts: 45,000 pairs of short sentences, styled like the CrowS-Pairs test.
#
- **[Queer People are People First: Deconstructing Sexual Identity Stereotypes in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00101)** ![english](https://img.shields.io/badge/language:-english-blue) ![real](https://img.shields.io/badge/type_of_answer:-full_answer-red) ![queer](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-queer_identity-green) 


> Prompts: Wikipedia biographies rewritten to remove explicit identity terms and gendered pronouns. An extra sentence is added stating that the person has a specific sexual identity.
> The model’s responses are compared to see how the identity changes the generated descriptions.

- **[Transphobia Is in the Eye of the Prompter: Trans-Centered Perspectives on Large Language Models](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3743676)** ![english](https://img.shields.io/badge/language:-english-blue)  ![real](https://img.shields.io/badge/type_of_answer:-full_answer-red) ![queer](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-queer_identity-green) 

> Prompts: Quora questions about trans people are asked to LLMs.

- **[Exploring LGBTQ+ Bias in Generative AI Answers across Different Country and Religious Contexts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03473)** ![english](https://img.shields.io/badge/language:-english-blue)  ![real](https://img.shields.io/badge/type_of_answer:-full_answer-red) ![queer](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-queer_identity-green) ![race](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-race-yellow) ![religion](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-religion-pink)

- **[Guardrails, not Guidance: Understanding Responses to  LGBTQ+ Language in Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.queerinai-main.2/)** ![english](https://img.shields.io/badge/language:-english-blue)  ![emotions](https://img.shields.io/badge/type_of_answer:-emotions-purple) ![queer](https://img.shields.io/badge/bias:-queer_identity-green) 

> Prompts: use of heteronormative vs. non-heteronormative language and the impact of LGBTQ+ slang. Heteronormative prompts are tied to **hegemonic heteronormativity** e.g., assumptions like rigid gender roles.
